Chocolate Cake

Indulge in this rich, moist, and delicious homemade chocolate cake that's perfect for birthdays, celebrations, or simply satisfying your sweet tooth. Made with simple pantry ingredients, this classic recipe is easy to follow and delivers a soft, fluffy cake with deep chocolate flavor.

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • ¼ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 cup milk
  • ½ cup hot coffee or hot water

Instructions

  1. Preheat the Oven
    • Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C).
    • Grease and lightly flour an 8-inch or 9-inch round cake pan.
  2. Mix the Dry Ingredients
    • In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
  3. Add the Wet Ingredients
    • Add the eggs, vegetable oil, vanilla extract, and milk.
    • Mix until the batter becomes smooth.
  4. Pour in Hot Liquid
    • Slowly stir in the hot coffee (or hot water).
    • The batter will be thin—this is normal.
  5. Bake
    • Pour the batter into the prepared cake pan.
    • Bake for 30–35 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  6. Cool
    • Let the cake cool in the pan for about 10 minutes.
    • Transfer it to a wire rack and allow it to cool completely.
  7. Decorate (Optional)
    • Frost with chocolate buttercream or ganache.
    • Garnish with chocolate shavings, berries, or sprinkles before serving.
